HRMS Feature Planning and Coordination

Harvtech Private Limited

1 / 5
The need

From spreadsheets to one system

HR records, attendance and payroll data were being maintained in MS Excel and separate documents. The HRMS aims to bring intern records, attendance, stipends and documents into one place.

Requirements I defined

Four modules, each with clear HR needs

Employee databaseSearch by name, ID, phone or email. Filter by department and status. One profile per intern.
AttendanceMonthly present and absent calendar, weekends excluded, with an attendance percentage per intern.
Stipend processingMonthly stipend per intern, paid or pending status, payment history and a pay-all option.
Document managementResume, offer letter, NDA and exit letter per intern, marked as uploaded or missing.
Prototype walkthrough

How the screens connect

Sign in→Dashboard→Employees→Intern profile→Attendance→Payroll→Settings

The dashboard shows total and active interns with a monthly trend. Each intern profile holds contact details, stipend and documents. Attendance and payroll are tracked month by month. This is a clickable prototype with demo data, not live company records.
